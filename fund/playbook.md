# Sklad

Trend, mean reversion, volatility breakout, relative value, cross-sectional momentum and funding-as-signal on crypto spot. Majors are BTC, ETH, SOL and BNB, plus liquid alts. Memecoins stay out unless every integrity and security field is present.

Capital is 1000 EUR, paper only. Risk per trade is at most 1% of the account. Size is account × 0.01 / distance to the stop, and never above 1× the account. Daily loss of 3% blocks new entries until the next UTC day. A 15% drop from the equity high is the kill switch: stops stay, new entries stop, and the fund waits for Aleks. Leverage is 1×. Shorts are off. No martingale, no averaging down, no widening a stop.

No exchange keys, no live orders, no withdrawals. Live mode does not exist in this code.

Costs in every test and every paper fill: 0.10% fee per side and 0.05% slippage per side. Missing data is a veto. Timestamps are UTC.
