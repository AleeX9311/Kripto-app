# Ideje

Each line is a reason to test, not a result. Rejected ideas stay here for a month.

- Trend: ride BTC when the fast average is above the slow one and price pulls back to it, because slow money keeps buying dips.
- Trend: same rule on ETH, one rule across markets rather than a new rule per coin.
- Trend: require a higher low so a falling knife is not called a pullback.
- Trend: skip the trade when 24h performance is already extended, because late entries pay the herd.
- Mean reversion: buy a washout under the 20-bar mean when the 50-bar mean is not falling, because forced sellers need a bid.
- Mean reversion: same washout on SOL, only if the stop still sits inside 0.8% to 6%.
- Mean reversion: do not buy a crash day, a falling 50-bar average is the losing side.
- Volatility breakout: enter only after a tight range breaks and the next bar retests, because the first print is often the trap.
- Volatility breakout: require rising volume on the break so a dead market does not count.
- Relative value: long the alt that beat BTC by 2% over 24h and still holds its own trend, because fragmented attention leaves a gap.
- Relative value: never apply that spread to BTC against itself.
- Cross-section: hold only the strongest liquid alt of the day, inside a moderate gain, because flows follow winners for days.
- Cross-section: drop the leader if it is already up more than 20% in a day.
- Funding: treat a very negative Hyperliquid funding print as a reason the longs are not crowded, and only with the trend already in place.
- Funding: a very positive funding print vetoes the long. It is a data point, not a short.
- Event: stand aside 30 minutes either side of CPI and FOMC, because the spread is the tax.
- Event: after the blackout, trade half size for the rest of the day, because the first move often reverses.
- Basis: spot versus perp basis is a research series only until a funded carry backtest exists. No short leg while shorts are off.
- Session: compare Europe open versus Asia for BTC trend continuation. One parameter, after 30 paper trades.
- Listings: a new Binance listing is an event, not an entry, until a full-cost test says otherwise.
- Memecoin: established pool, age, volume and reserve present, and every security field present, or no trade.
- Memecoin: a missing bundle figure is a veto, not a pass.
- Delistings: keep delisted symbols in the research set so the test is not only today's survivors.
- Gap: two venues printing different prices is a research idea. No second venue is wired, so it is not a trade.
